Privacy Policy

Stokit uses the information you provide to power household pantry tracking, shared grocery lists, receipts, store reminders, and related app features.

The app may request access to your camera for barcode and receipt scanning, photos for receipt attachments, and location for store arrival reminders. This information is used only to provide the requested app functionality.

We do not sell personal information. For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact qadeeradil14@gmail.com.
