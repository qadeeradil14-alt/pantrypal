# Stokit App Store Screenshot Direction

## Selected direction

Dark green premium.

This is the approved visual direction for Stokit App Store screenshots.

## Design rules

Use:
- Dark green premium background
- Coral Stokit accent
- One feature per screenshot
- Real app UI inside an iPhone frame
- Large headline
- Short subtitle
- Clean Stokit branding
- Premium, calm, modern grocery-tech feel

Avoid:
- Wide banner as final App Store upload
- Tablet-style layout
- Too many phones in one screenshot
- Tiny unreadable text
- Fake app UI
- Personal data
- OTA/debug labels in final screenshots
- Real private addresses if avoidable

## Final App Store screenshot set

Final upload should be six separate iPhone portrait screenshots, not one wide banner.

Recommended order:

1. Plan your grocery trips  
   Subtitle: All your lists and stores, in one place.  
   Screen: Pantry/home

2. Shop at your favorite stores  
   Subtitle: Add stores you shop at and assign items easily.  
   Screen: Stores tab

3. Know where every item goes  
   Subtitle: Assign groceries to the right store in one tap.  
   Screen: Store assignment sheet

4. Shop store by store  
   Subtitle: Follow your list, check off items, and stay on track.  
   Screen: Shopping trip screen

5. Remember what you paid  
   Subtitle: Add prices once. Stokit remembers for next time.  
   Screen: Spend / price memory screen

6. See your progress  
   Subtitle: Finish each stop and keep your trip moving.  
   Screen: Stop complete / trip progress screen

## Folder purpose

- `concepts/` = visual direction and generated concepts
- `raw/` = real screenshots captured from Stokit
- `final/` = final App Store-ready exported screenshots
