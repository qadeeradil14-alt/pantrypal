## Usage Guidelines

### ✓ DO
- Maintain 20px clear space around entire logo
- Scale proportionally (width:height ratio locked)
- Use primary color combination for best recognition
- Apply monogram for favicon (120px+)
- Print single-color version in black on white backgrounds

### ✗ DON'T
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Change colors without brand approval
- Use below 120px without simplification
- Apply drop shadows or effects
- Place on busy backgrounds (use solid color)

### Minimum Sizes
- Full horizontal lockup: 200px wide
- Icon only: 120px square
- Monogram: 80px square (favicon 32px with antialiasing)

## Design Rationale

**Concept:** Minimalist geometric bowl containing an apple—represents organized pantry storage and fresh food.

**Typography:** Inter Bold (sans-serif) pairs with organic bowl shape for balance between modern and approachable.

**Color:** Warm brown + terracotta creates sophisticated, natural aesthetic matching PantryPal's earthy design system. Stands out against cream backgrounds; works across digital and print.

**Scalability:** Clean geometric forms ensure logo reads at all sizes—from favicon to billboard.

**Versatility:** Icon-first design means logo works as standalone mark, with text, or in badge format without redesign.
